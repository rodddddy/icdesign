    Assumptions & model limits

      Before using these estimates

      • These are reduced, linear small-signal approximations around an assumed operating point. Only the poles, zeros and parasitics explicitly represented by the equations are included.
      • All MOS devices are assumed to operate in saturation. Supply voltage, bias currents, headroom, output swing and saturation are not solved or verified here.
      • Body effect is neglected in all eight models. Verify body connections and device operating points separately.
      • Bias voltages and current sources are idealized except for explicitly modeled small-signal resistance. Bias generation, mismatch, noise and large-signal behavior are outside scope.
      • Differential models use the signal and output convention stated in their equations. Common-mode feedback (CMFB), common-mode operating point and CMFB-loop stability are not designed or checked.
      • Use operating-point analysis and transistor-level simulation to validate a real design. This calculator does not claim transistor-level SPICE accuracy.
